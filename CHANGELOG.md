## 0.1.0

Initial release preparation.

- Spreadsheet-style CSV and TSV editing with progressive row loading.
- Filters, numeric/text sorting, pinned rows and columns, and selection statistics.
- Inline editing, copy with or without headers, batch operations, Undo and Redo.
- Aligned CSV Text mode and block editing.
- Save, Save As, view export, and recovery backups.
- Light/dark themes, English/Simplified Chinese, and guided help.
